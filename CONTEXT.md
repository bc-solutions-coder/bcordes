# bcordes

A public portfolio and inquiry site connected to its organization in Wallow.

## Language

**bcordes organization**:
The organization in Wallow that owns bcordes’ registered applications and organization-scoped data.
_Avoid_: tenant, workspace

**bcordes developer application**:
The application registered to the bcordes organization through which people sign in to bcordes.
_Avoid_: first-party Wallow application

**Wallow identity provider**:
The identity provider at wallow.dev through which people authenticate to bcordes.

**Customer**:
A person who submits inquiries and can access and comment on their own inquiries after signing in.

**Staff**:
People responsible for managing the bcordes organization’s inquiries, including their statuses.
